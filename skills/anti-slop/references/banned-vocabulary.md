# Banned Vocabulary

Words and phrases that mark text as LLM-generated on sight. The smell is the same everywhere: a word chosen because it sounds like expertise rather than because it communicates. Use the word you would use out loud to a colleague.

## The Single Worst Offender

**load-bearing** — Say what actually depends on it. "This config is load-bearing" tells the reader nothing. "The retry loop reads this config on every attempt" tells them what breaks if they touch it.

| realm | domain, area, or cut entirely |
| ecosystem (outside software) | community, system |

## Significance Inflation

Phrases that inflate importance without adding information.

- "a testament to" → shows, demonstrates
- "stands as" → is
- "serves as" → is, works as
- "plays a crucial role" → matters, affects
- "underscores the importance of" → shows that X matters
- "marks a shift" → changes, moves
- "at its core" → fundamentally, basically, or cut
- "the reality is" → — (just state the fact)
- "it's worth noting" → — (if worth noting, just note it)
- "fundamentally" → — (usually cut)
- "profound" → significant, large, or cut
- "transformative" → — (say what it transforms)

## Transition Scaffolding

Words that signal a transition but add no meaning.

- moreover → and, also, or new sentence
- furthermore → and, also
- additionally → also, and
- notably → — (if notable, just say it)
- importantly → — (cut)
- "that said" (paragraph opener) → but, however
- "in today's fast-paced X" → — (cut entirely)
- "when it comes to" → for, about, regarding
- "let's dive in" → — (cut)

## Hedge-and-Flatter Openers

- "Great question" → — (just answer)
- "You're absolutely right" → — (just add your point)
- "I hope this finds you well" → — (cut)
- "Certainly!" → — (just do the thing)
- "Absolutely." → yes, or just do the thing

## The Hallway Test

Beyond this list, the smell is the same everywhere. If a sentence would survive being said to a colleague in a hallway, it's fine. Ordinary verbs (has, uses, breaks, runs, needs, sends) beat elevated ones every time.
